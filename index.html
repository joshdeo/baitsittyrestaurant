Welcome to Bait Sitty

A taste of home and tradition.

We bring back the warmth and flavors of our grandmothers’ kitchens, where every meal is made with love, patience and authentic homemade recipes. Here, tradition meets comfort, and sharing food means sharing memories, laughter and the true taste of home.

The full menu is halal certified.

Because in our culture, food is love. And at Bait Sitty, love is served generously, just like Sitty always did.

Bait Sitty means grandma’s house. It is where the soulful warmth of home cooking comes to life, where tradition, love and the art of comforting food gather at one table.

You may arrive as a guest, but you are welcomed as family, just as Sitty welcomed everyone who stepped into her home. There is comfort in every plate, generosity in every serving and love woven into every recipe.

Come sit, savor and make memories

Stepping into Bait Sitty feels like walking into your grandmother’s warm embrace. The charm of old world Arab homes meets a refreshed, modern touch: soft earthy tones, warm wood, embroidered details and gentle hints of brass and lace.
