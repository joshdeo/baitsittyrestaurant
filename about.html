About Bait Sitty

A timeless grandmother inspired dining experience.

Bait Sitty is where the soulful warmth of home cooking comes to life. Where tradition, love and the art of comforting food gather at one table. It is a journey back to the flavors of childhood, to the scent of slow cooked meals filling the house and the sound of family gathering before the first bite is served.

Here, food is more than taste. It is memory, emotion and a story passed down from grandmother’s hands to ours. Bait Sitty nourishes not only the body but the heart, reminding us that the greatest meals were never rushed and the most unforgettable flavors were born in a humble family kitchen.

At Bait Sitty, you may arrive as a guest, but you are welcomed as family, just as Sitty welcomed everyone who stepped into her home. There is comfort in every plate, generosity in every serving and love woven into every recipe, just as she taught us.

Inspired by generations of home cooked tradition, our dishes celebrate authenticity, patience and the beauty of simple, honest ingredients. Each plate carries the warmth of grandmother’s touch: a taste of heritage, a whisper of nostalgia and the embrace only home can give.

Because in our culture, food is love. And at Bait Sitty, love is served generously, just like Sitty always did.

Ambience

Stepping into Bait Sitty feels like walking into your grandmother’s warm embrace. A place where time slows down, stories live in every corner and comfort fills the air. The charm of old world Arab homes meets a refreshed, modern touch: soft earthy tones, warm wood, embroidered details and gentle hints of brass and lace that echo memories of family gatherings and lovingly prepared meals.

Come sit, savor and make memories, just as we always did at Grandma’s house.

Here, you don’t just dine. You belong.
